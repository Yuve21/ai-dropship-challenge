## Asset requirement
| Asset | Exists? | Produced by |
|---|---|---|
| 3 text cards, high contrast, one line each | **Must be produced.** ~10 minutes | Canva MCP (`docs/AUTOMATION.md`), or the phone's own video editor |
| Ambient household B-roll under the cards: a tap running, a bowl being filled, light on a kitchen counter | **Must be filmed**, or omitted entirely and run on plain cards | Founder's phone, 60 seconds of filming |

This is the lowest-asset script in the bank. It runs with **zero** footage if it has to: three text cards
over a plain background is a complete, postable video. That makes it the fallback whenever a day's filming
does not happen, and the reason it sits early in the week.

## The idea, one line
A short list video that is genuinely useful about cat water habits, ending on the honest fact that we sell
the plug-in version of the third item. No demo, because there is nothing to demo yet.

> **AMENDED 2026-08-13 by `challenge-lead`.** This script was written in a first-person cat-owner voice
> ("my cat", "she was not being fussy", "the bowl she uses least"). **The founder has no cat**
> (`tracker/LEDGER.md`, 2026-08-13), so every one of those lines was a fabricated household observation.
> Re-voiced to the account's actual position: a **finds and curation account** reporting what cat owners
> commonly report, which is true, checkable and requires no pet. The title always said "Three Things Cat
> Owners Get Wrong"; only the card copy had drifted into first person.

## Hook (first 3 seconds, the opening VISUAL frame)
Card 1 on screen at 0.0s, large type, no build: "three things people get wrong about cats and water." Text
is the visual. Sound-off legible by construction.

## Shot list (13s)
| Time | Beat | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Hook card | Full-frame text card, high contrast. | "three things people get wrong about cats and water" |
| 2-5s | One | Card over B-roll of a bowl being filled, or plain card. | "1. a full bowl is not the same as a used bowl" |
| 5-8s | Two | Card over B-roll of a tap, or plain card. | "2. a cat at the tap is not being fussy. moving water is just what a lot of cats go for" |
| 8-11s | Three | Card, plain. | "3. the usual advice is to keep the water away from the food bowl" |
| 11-13s | Turn + loop | Card, then cut back to the hook card. | "so we started a shop that sells one thing, a fountain, and it is not here yet" |

**Every item is now stated as general cat-owner knowledge, not as something observed in this household.**
That is the only honest register available to this account, and it is also the register a "finds" account
should be in anyway. Item 3 is phrased as "the usual advice is" rather than as a fact we verified, because
the food-bowl-proximity point is widely repeated advice rather than something we measured. **Do not
re-personalise any of these lines.** If a future editor wants a first-person observation in this slot, the
observation has to be true of a real household, and this one has no cat in it.

## Hook variants (one concept, three hooks, body and CTA frozen)
1. "three things people get wrong about cats and water." **(the version scheduled)**
2. "nobody tells you this about cats and water bowls."
3. "if your cat's water bowl looks untouched every night, read this."

All three are addressed to the viewer's cat, never to ours. That is deliberate and is the pattern every
script on this account now follows.

## Format recommendation
Text cards, produced in Canva. This is the organic cousin of the text-only static ad spec
(`creative/scripts/static-ad-1-text-only-still-bowl.md`), and the two are deliberately separate: the static
finding (text-only ads win most often) is **Meta paid data** and does not transfer to TikTok organic, where
our own brief records 300+ scheduled image posts producing nothing. This script is a video with text in it,
posted at video cadence, not an image post. Do not read a win here as validation of the paid finding, or
the other way round.

## Compliance notes
- **Every list item is general cat-owner knowledge, attributed as such, never a household observation.**
  This account has no cat and may not claim one, in any register, including implication
  (`compliance/no-sample-copy-audit-2026-08-12.md` sets the same standard for product claims). No health,
  hydration-as-medicine, or veterinary claim.
- No claim about the product's quietness, durability or ease of cleaning. The only product facts stated are
  price and the honest shipping window.
- No invented statistic. There is no "cats drink 40% more" line in this script and there must not be one:
  we have measured nothing.
- No income claims, royalty-free audio, no bare link.
