Protocol: Challenge Warm-Up Protocol v1, Track A, Day 3 (2026-08-20). Product content and CTA allowed.

directly about the product, and it does so by naming exactly what we do not know.

**This is the one place the Dashboard-and-Pointer recipe still fully applies with no product in hand**
(`docs/LEARNINGS.md`, 2026-08-12). The dashboard here is our own store page, which is a screen we control
and can legitimately screen-record: five elements intact, screen recording, real hand pointing, one bright
overlay, original audio, caption under six words. The physical-product adaptation of that recipe is what
the no-sample start breaks; the original software version of it is not broken at all.

## Hook (first 3 seconds, the opening VISUAL frame)
The store page already on screen, scrolling, with a hand entering frame within the first second. Motion and
a human hand immediately. No face.

## Shot list, one unbroken take (11s)
| Time | What happens | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Screen recording of our own product page, mid-scroll, hand entering frame. | none yet |
| 2-4s | Scroll stops on the price. Finger points at $34.99. | none yet |
| 4-7s | Scroll to the shipping line. Finger holds on it. Bright overlay appears over that line. | **"7-20 BUSINESS DAYS"** (the one overlay) |
| 7-9s | Finger stays. Scroll pauses on the empty reviews area. | none |
| 9-11s | Back to the top of the page to loop. | none |

One overlay only, per the recipe. The overlay names the least flattering fact on the page on purpose: the
slow shipping window is the thing a viewer would otherwise find out at checkout, and putting it on screen
is both the honest move and, per `docs/LEARNINGS.md` on extra costs and delivery surprise being the largest
fixable abandonment causes, probably the converting one.

## Spoken line (original audio, optional but recommended)
"New shop, one product. It ships in seven to twenty business days, which is slow, and I am telling you that
here instead of at checkout. Mine has not arrived yet either, so there is no demo video on this account. I
am not going to fake one."

## CTA rung
Rung 1 (pinned comment). Verbatim: "shop name typed below, one product, $34.99, 7-20 business days. no
review from me yet because I have not held it. when it lands it goes on camera either way."

## Comment-bait element
Open-ended and slightly provocative, which is the point: "comment the longest you have ever waited for
something you ordered online." No product ownership required, no cat required, and it invites the
shipping-time objection into the open where it can be answered rather than leaving it to sink the
conversion silently.

**Watch this one.** If the comments turn into a pile-on about slow shipping rather than an ordinary
conversation, that is a real signal about the offer and belongs in `docs/LEARNINGS.md`, not something to
edit away quietly.

## Format recommendation
Real screen recording, real hand. Nothing here is generated. Note the audience risk, stated plainly: a
build-in-public video attracts people interested in shops, not people who want a cat fountain, which is the
exact transfer caveat `docs/LEARNINGS.md` flags about the source account for this format (their audience
was aspiring dropshippers, ours is meant to be cat owners). Judge this one on **buy-intent comments and
profile taps, not views**, and cap the family at one post every two days regardless of how well it
performs on views.

## Compliance notes
- **This script depends on the store existing.** Do not film a screen recording of a mockup, a competitor's
  page, or a page that is not live, and do not caption a draft page as if it were the live store.
- Shipping window quoted exactly as the shipping policy states it (`store/copy/policy-shipping.md`,
  7-20 business days per `ops/sourcing-dossier.md`). The screen and the spoken line must match the page.
  If the policy page's placeholders are filled with different numbers before this films, update this script
  to match the page, not the other way round.
- Empty reviews area is shown, not hidden, and never filled with placeholder review text
  (`store/copy/product-cat-water-fountain.md` already forbids that).
- No health claims, no income claims, no "make money with me" framing. This is a shop being honest about
  its own logistics, not a business-opportunity post, and it must not drift into one.
