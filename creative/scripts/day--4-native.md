# Day -4 Native Post: "She's decided I'm not allowed to get up"

Account: @dailypawfinds
Protocol: Challenge Warm-Up Protocol v1, Track A, Day -4 (`docs/WARM-UP.md`)
Post type: native, non-promotional. No product, no link, no CTA, no brand pitch of any kind.

## Concept, one line
A relatable pet-owner moment: getting pinned on the couch or bed by a cat who has claimed your lap, and the small internal negotiation that follows.

## Script / shot list (11-13s)

| Time | Shot | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Handheld, POV looking down at your own lap: cat is curled up, fully settled, eyes maybe half-closed. | This is the hook. Show the "trapped" state immediately, don't build up to it. |
| 2-4s | Cut to your hand reaching for something just out of reach (phone, remote, glass of water on the side table) and stopping short. | Small, honest gesture. No acting needed, just reach and hesitate. |
| 4-7s | Cut back to the cat, close on the face, maybe one eye opens and closes again. | This is the "she knows" beat. |
| 7-10s | Wide-ish shot: you settling back into the couch, giving up, hand resting on the cat instead. | Resolution beat, low energy, real. |
| 10-13s | Loop-friendly close: back to the same POV-down-at-lap framing as the opening shot. | Lets the video loop cleanly into the hook again. |

## On-screen text (burned-in captions, since this has to work sound off)
- 0-2s: "when she falls asleep on you and you have things to do"
- 4-7s: "she knows"
- 10-13s: "guess I'm not getting up"

## Suggested caption
the things we do for a sleeping cat. royalty-free audio, trending sound optional if one fits the beat without needing music rights.

## Why this is genuinely native, not a disguised ad
There is nothing to buy in this video and nothing points at anything to buy. It's the single most common cat-owner post format on the platform: "I am now furniture." No product is filmed, no brand is named, no caption drives anywhere. A real cat owner posts this exact video with zero commercial intent, which is the bar for this slot in the warm-up protocol.

## Format recommendation
**Real phone-filmed UGC, not AI-generated.** This entire video depends on one specific, small, unscripted-looking cat behavior (the half-open eye, the real weight of a real cat) reading as authentic. AI-generated pet footage is currently easy to spot as fake in exactly these subtle, low-motion moments, and a fake-looking cat video during warm-up (before there's any reason to fake one) has no upside. Film it with an actual cat on an actual couch.

## Compliance notes
- No health/medical claims, no income claims, no before/after framing, no copyrighted music (use royalty-free audio or a licensed/trending TikTok sound cleared for commercial use later).
- No product, no link, no CTA, no brand mention anywhere in video or caption. Confirmed clean for Day -4.
