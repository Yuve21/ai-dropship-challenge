## Concept, one line
A ranked list of the five places a cat will actually pick in an ordinary room, filmed as five empty spots
in a real home, with the joke being that none of them is anything you bought.

## Hook (first 3 seconds, the opening VISUAL frame, not the caption)
A bright sunlit patch on bare floor or carpet, shot low and close, dust visible in the light. Empty. The
text card lands immediately over it. **The absence of a cat is the format, not a compromise:** the viewer
is being asked to picture their own cat in each spot, which is the whole reason the list works.

## Script / shot list (13-15s)

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Low, close on a sunlit patch of empty floor. Hold still, no pan. | "five spots a cat picks in a room like this" |
| 2-4s | Tilt or cut up to the highest flat surface in the room: top of a bookshelf, a wardrobe, the fridge. Empty. | "1. the highest flat thing in the room" |
| 4-6s | A plain cardboard box on the floor, flaps open. Any box. | "2. any box. any size. immediately" |
| 6-8s | Whatever the founder is genuinely using right now: open laptop, an open book face down, the warm router. | "3. whatever you are currently using" |
| 8-10s | A basket or stack of clean folded laundry. | "4. the clean laundry. obviously the clean laundry" |
| 10-12s | Back to the sunlit floor patch, slightly wider. | "5. the sun. it is always the sun" |
| 12-15s | Loop-friendly close: the exact opening framing again. | "none of these cost anything" |

**Shooting note:** lock the phone off for each shot, do not pan, and hold each one a beat longer than feels
necessary so the text has room to be read. Five static shots cut together at 2 seconds each is the entire
edit.

## Why this is genuinely native, not a disguised ad
Nothing is for sale in it, nothing is named, nothing is linked, and the caption drives nowhere. It is the
standard cat-owner list format, posted constantly by people with no commercial relationship to anything,
because it is a joke that lands on its own. It also fits the account bio exactly: a finds and observation
account making an observation.

## Format recommendation
**Real phone footage of a real room, plus burned-in text cards.** Two reasons, and the second one is the
important one. First, text-on-screen is the highest-winner-hit-rate format we have documented evidence for
(11.60% text-only against 7.56% UGC, Motion, `docs/LEARNINGS.md` 2026-08-10), and although that evidence is
**Meta paid data and does not transfer to TikTok organic**, this is a video with text in it posted at video
cadence, not an image post, so the organic objection to statics does not apply either. Second, an empty
room is filmable today by a person with a phone and nothing else, which is the binding constraint.

**Do not generate any part of this.** No generated cat, no generated room. There is no cat in the script,
and generating one to "improve" it would be the exact fabrication this whole plan refuses.

## AIGC label state
**Not applicable, real footage.** If posted through the CLI, pass `--no-aigc` explicitly: `automation/cli.js`
defaults `isAigc` to true, so a real-footage post sent without the flag goes out mislabelled.

## What this version costs against the original, honestly
The original had a live animal doing a small unscripted thing in the first two seconds, which is the
strongest opening frame available in this niche and the reason it was written. This version opens on an
empty floor. **We are trading a live-animal hook for a list structure**, and a list structure has a lower
ceiling on a cold account with no followers. There is no number to attach to that gap, because we have zero
posts and nobody publishes an organic winner rate. It is stated so the Day -4 read is not misinterpreted as
a verdict on the format when it may be a verdict on the constraint.
