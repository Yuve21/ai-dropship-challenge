## Concept, one line
A pet-behavior fact/observation video: why cats knead soft surfaces ("making biscuits"), a well-known, uncontroversial behavior explanation, filmed while it's actually happening.

## Script / shot list (10-14s)

| Time | Shot | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Close, overhead-ish angle on a cat's paws actively kneading a blanket. | Hook is the behavior itself, already in motion, no wind-up. |
| 2-5s | Cut wider to show the whole cat, eyes closed or half-closed, clearly content. | Establishes context without narration needed. |
| 5-9s | Continue the paw close-up while on-screen text explains the fact. | This is the "content" beat, carried by text, not a talking-head. |

| 12-14s | Loop-friendly close: back to the paw close-up from the opening shot. | Clean loop back into the hook. |

## On-screen text (burned-in captions, this video is carried by text)
- 0-2s: "if your cat does this, here's why"
- 5-9s: "it's leftover from kittenhood, kneading a mother cat while nursing helps milk flow, they just never grow out of the instinct"
- 9-12s: "it's a comfort behavior, not a bad habit"
- 12-14s: "she still does it as a fully grown cat, every night"

## Suggested caption
the biscuit-making explained. royalty-free audio, or a trending sound with no lyrics so the captions stay legible.

## Why this is genuinely native, not a disguised ad
This is straightforward pet-education content, the kind of fact-based video that drives a large share of organic reach in the pet niche on its own, with nothing to sell attached. No product appears in frame, no brand is mentioned, and the fact stated (kneading as a kittenhood-nursing instinct) is a widely accepted behavioral explanation, not a claim about any product or health outcome.

## Format recommendation
**Real phone-filmed UGC.** The paw-kneading close-up is a very specific, textured motion that's simple to film with a real cat and hard to make look convincing if generated. Since the entire hook is "watch this happen," authenticity of the motion matters more than production polish. Film it in a normal lighting moment when the cat is already doing this, no staging needed.
