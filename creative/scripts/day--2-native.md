Two hard rules, and they are not negotiable:

1. **Verify and record the actual licence and source URL for every clip, in the table below, before the
   clip enters an edit.** A clip with an empty row does not get posted. "It was on a free site" is not a

## Concept, one line
A short, uncontroversial explainer of why cats knead soft surfaces, carried by text over licensed footage of
the behaviour, addressed to the viewer's cat and never to ours.

## Hook (first 3 seconds, the opening VISUAL frame)
Clip A at 0.0s: paws already mid-knead on a blanket, close, motion in frame immediately. No wind-up, no
title card first. The behaviour is the hook.

## Script / shot list (12-14s)

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Clip A, close on the paws, already kneading. | "if your cat does this on a blanket, here is why" |
| 2-5s | Clip B, wider, whole cat, eyes closed or half closed. | (no text, let the shot breathe) |
| 5-9s | Back to Clip A, or hold Clip B, whichever is steadier. | "it is left over from kittenhood. kneading while nursing helps the milk come" |
| 9-12s | Clip B, second moment or a different section of the same clip. | "they just never grow out of it. it is a comfort thing, not a bad habit" |
| 12-14s | Loop-friendly close: back to the exact Clip A opening frame. | "so they are basically saying they are comfortable" |

## Hook variants (one concept, three hooks, body frozen)
1. "if your cat does this on a blanket, here is why" over the paws close-up. **(scheduled 08-16)**
2. "there is an actual reason for the biscuit making" over the same frame.

**Licensed stock footage plus burned-in text.** This is the one native slot where stock earns its place:

bans generated product, cat and person, and with no real footage of our own the temptation to generate one
is exactly the trap that ban exists for. Abstract water, textures and motion are the only generated assets
this account uses.
