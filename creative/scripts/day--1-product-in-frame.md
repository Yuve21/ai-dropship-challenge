Post type: last unpromotional post before real posting starts. The cat water fountain may appear **incidentally** in frame. Still no pitch, no CTA, no link, no "check this out," no product naming, no lingering shot on the product.

## Concept, one line
A native, funny/relatable "cat ignoring you for the fourth toy today" moment filmed in the kitchen, where the fountain happens to be visible on the counter in the background, running, exactly like any other kitchen appliance would be.

## Script / shot list (11-13s)

| Time | Shot | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Handheld, medium shot in the kitchen: cat sniffing/batting at a toy on the floor, person crouched down trying to get the cat's attention with a different toy or their hand. | Hook is the relatable moment, not the product. Kitchen counter with the fountain running is naturally in the background of this shot, not framed or centered. |
| 2-5s | Cat ignores the person, wanders off toward the fountain area (natural cat movement toward water it's used to), fountain briefly more visible mid-frame as the cat passes it. | The fountain should read as "just a thing on the counter," in the same visual weight as anything else back there. No zoom, no pause, no focus pull toward it. |
| 5-9s | Cat sits down near the counter, person continues talking to / gesturing at the cat, still trying to get attention with the toy. | Fountain may be partially in frame here, out of focus or at the edge, never the subject. |
| 9-11s | Person gives up, sits back, cat still uninterested. | Resolution beat. |
| 11-13s | Loop-friendly close: back to the opening framing, cat and toy on the floor. | Mirrors the hook for a clean loop. |

## Why this stays compliant with the Day -1 rule
The product is visible the way any real object in a real kitchen would be visible: unstaged, unmentioned, not the reason the camera is there. The video's actual subject, start to finish, is the toy-ignoring bit. This is the incidental-in-frame allowance the protocol describes, not a soft pitch. If in doubt while filming: if the fountain shot were cropped out entirely, the video would be identical in meaning. That's the bar for "incidental."

## Format recommendation
**Real phone-filmed UGC.** This needs to look like an unremarkable clip from an ordinary kitchen, which is exactly what a real phone camera produces by default. Staging this with AI generation would risk making the product placement look intentional or overly clean, the opposite of "incidental."
