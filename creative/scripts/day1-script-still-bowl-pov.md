## The idea, one line
Show the problem in the viewer's kitchen, not the solution in ours. The whole video is a bowl that nobody
drank from, which is a thing every cat owner already recognises and which costs us nothing to film.

## Hook (first 3 seconds, the opening VISUAL frame)
Overhead close on a full, perfectly still bowl of water. No motion at all for a full beat. The stillness is
the hook; the format is the same tension trick as `day4-script-silent-comparison.md`, minus the payoff cut
we cannot film yet.

## Shot list (11s)
| Time | Beat | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Hook | Overhead, full still bowl, no movement, natural morning light. | "filled it at 8am" |
| 2-5s | Same frame, later | Identical framing, evening light, water level visibly unchanged. Same angle, same crop, so the only difference is the light. | "same bowl. 6pm." |
| 5-8s | Recognition | Cat walks through frame past the bowl without stopping, or if she will not cooperate, hold on the bowl. | "she has walked past it maybe forty times" |
