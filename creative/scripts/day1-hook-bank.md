# Day 1 Hook Bank: Cat Water Fountain

Account: @dailypawfinds
Protocol: Challenge Warm-Up Protocol v1, Track A, Day 1 onward (`docs/WARM-UP.md`). Full product content and CTA allowed starting Day 1.
Product: cat water fountain, ABS/plastic housing, pump-circulated, quiet motor, $34.99, easy to clean.

## Claims that are allowed and claims that are banned

**Allowed, and true:**
- Cats are commonly drawn to moving water over still water. This is a widely accepted behavioral observation, not a medical claim.
- The motor is quiet.
- It's easy to clean / comes apart for cleaning.

**Banned in every script, no exceptions:**
- Any claim that this prevents, treats, reduces risk of, or relates to UTIs, kidney disease, kidney failure, or any other medical condition. This category of claim is extremely common in this product's marketing everywhere else. It does not appear anywhere in this account's content.
- Any health claim, any income claim, any before/after framing.
- A bare link in the caption. The account has not cleared the ~1,000-follower bio-link gate. CTA goes to the TikTok Shop listing or a pinned/typed comment only.

**Production note for every script below:** royalty-free audio only, no copyrighted or unlicensed commercial track. Every script must be legible with sound off, meaning the claim, the CTA, and the "wow" all have to live in burned-in on-screen text, not narration alone.

7. **"This has been running in my kitchen for three weeks and I still catch her staring at it."** Visual: cat sitting completely still, fixated on the moving water.
8. **"Nobody told me cats would rather drink from a fountain than a bowl. This one proved it in a day."** Visual: side-by-side-in-time (not before/after transformation, just sequence) of the old bowl sitting untouched, cut immediately to the cat drinking from the fountain.

**Notes on using this bank:**

- Hooks 2 and 6 are sound-off-first hooks, good for testing whether the account's audience is scrolling muted.
- Do not stack more than one hook claim in a single video. One hook, one body, one CTA.

## Format recommendation for this whole bank

**Real phone-filmed UGC is the better fit here, not AI-generated video, and it isn't close.** Every hook and both full scripts depend on a real cat's specific, small, unscripted-looking reaction to the product (staring at moving water, walking over unprompted, a tail flick, sleeping calmly next to a running motor). That is exactly the category of subtle animal behavior that currently reads as fake when AI-generated, and if it reads as fake, the whole "proof" beat collapses along with the CTA that follows it. AI generation could be used later for pure product-detail inserts (a clean rotating shot of the fountain alone, no animal, no behavior claim riding on it) if a fast turnaround is ever needed, but the hook and proof beats that carry this product should stay real, camera-in-hand footage of an actual cat with an actual fountain.

## CTA rule, restated so it isn't missed while filming
Every script's CTA text says "pinned comment" or "shop tab," never a spoken or written URL in the caption. This holds until the account clears the roughly 1,000-follower bio-link gate (`docs/WARM-UP.md`, section on the link gate). Update this file the day that gate clears, not before.
