## The idea, one line
Say the thing every viewer in this niche has half-noticed, out loud, in a real voice, over real footage of
our own kitchen, and let naming the shop at the end be the only commercial beat.

## Hook (first 3 seconds, the opening VISUAL frame)
The untouched bowl, close, with the first spoken line landing over it immediately. No greeting, no "hey
guys," no face required.

## Shot list (14s)
| Time | Beat | Shot | Voiceover | On-screen text (burned in, sound-off legible) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Hook | Still, full bowl, close. | "Your cat is not being dramatic about the water bowl." | "she is not being dramatic" |
| 3-6s | Problem | Cat at the tap, or the tap running alone. | "A lot of cats will walk past a full bowl all day and then drink out of a running tap." | "full bowl, ignored. running tap, fine." |

| 12-14s | Loop | Back to the still bowl frame. | "Ours has not arrived yet. When it does you will see it here either way." | "not here yet" |

Real footage plus the founder's real voice. If the founder will not record a voiceover, the fallback is

the wrong place to spend our credibility.
