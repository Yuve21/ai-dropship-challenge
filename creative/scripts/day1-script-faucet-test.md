## The idea, one line
The product thesis (a lot of cats prefer moving water to still water) is demonstrable with a kitchen tap.
We do not need the fountain to prove the category, only to prove our specific unit, which we are not
claiming anything about.

## Hook (first 3 seconds, the opening VISUAL frame, not the caption)
Cat's tongue already in the tap stream, filmed close, water clearly moving. Motion in frame at 0.0s. No
wind-up, no person talking first.

## Shot list (12s)
| Time | Beat | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Hook | Extreme close: cat drinking directly from a thin running tap stream. | "she does this every single day" |
| 2-4s | Problem | Cut to her full water bowl on the floor, untouched, still. Hold long enough to read the stillness. | "her actual bowl. full since this morning" |
| 4-7s | The point | Back to the tap. Hand turns the tap off. Cat waits, stares at the dry spout, does not go to the bowl. | "the water is right there. she wants it moving" |
| 7-10s | Honest line | Tap back on, cat drinks again. | "a lot of cats are like this, it is a known cat thing" |
| 10-12s | Loop | Cut back to the opening tongue-in-stream frame. | "so we are selling the obvious fix" |
