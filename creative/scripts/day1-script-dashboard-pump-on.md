## The five Dashboard-and-Pointer elements, adapted for a physical product
1. The product doing its trick, filmed as **one continuous unedited shot** (no cuts inside the take).
2. A real finger physically pointing at the exact moment it works.
3. One bright, highlighted 1-3 word overlay naming that moment.
4. Original audio (the real sound of the pump and water), no trending sound, no voiceover.
5. Caption under six words, plus 5-10 hashtags.

## Hook (first 3 seconds, the opening VISUAL frame, not the caption)
Camera already close on the fountain's still top tier, reservoir freshly filled, before power. A thumb is
visible at the frame edge, already on the plug. The hook is the moment the water goes from motionless to
moving, and it lands inside the first 2 seconds of the single take. Nobody speaks. The caption is not the
hook, this frame is.

## Shot list, one unbroken take (no internal cuts, no B-roll spliced in)
| Time | What happens | Camera |
|---|---|---|
| 0-1.5s | Still water on the top tier, reservoir already filled. Thumb visible at frame edge, on the plug. | Locked-down close angle, phone held steady, frame does not move. |
| 1.5s | Plug goes in. Pump engages. Water visibly starts moving across the top tier. | Same frame, no cut, no zoom. |
| 2-5s | A hand enters frame, one finger extended, points directly at the spot where the water just started moving. Hold the point. | Same frame. |
| 5-8s | Bright overlay text appears over the frame at the pointed spot, timed to the point (see below). | Same frame, overlay only, camera does not move. |
| 8-11s | Finger stays in frame. If the cat is nearby and walks in toward the water on its own, let it, don't cut to follow it. If not, hold on the water and the pointing hand to 11s and stop. | Same frame throughout. Stop recording; do not cut and splice a second clip in afterward. |

## On-screen text overlay (the only one, bright, high-contrast)
"WATER: ON" appears at 2s, timed to the finger point, held through the rest of the clip.

## Spoken or captioned line
None, by design. No voiceover, no burned-in caption beyond the one overlay above. This is a deliberate
departure from the account's other product scripts, which lean on dense burned-in captions for sound-off
legibility (`creative/scripts/day1-hook-bank.md`). The one overlay has to carry the meaning sound-off on its
own: still water, a finger, "WATER: ON," moving water. If this reads as less legible sound-off than the
standard-UGC family in the same week's read, that is itself a finding for `docs/LEARNINGS.md`.

## Format recommendation
Real phone-filmed footage. This format's entire credibility rests on the "unedited" read; an AI-generated
version would have to fake continuous causality (still water, plug, moving water, one uncut take), which is
exactly the kind of physical detail AI video currently fails to render convincingly. Film for real, no edits
inside the take.
