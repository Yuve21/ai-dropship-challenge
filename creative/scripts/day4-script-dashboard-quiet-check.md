## The five Dashboard-and-Pointer elements, adapted for a physical product
1. The product doing its trick, one continuous unedited shot.
2. A real finger pointing at the exact moment it works.
3. One bright 1-3 word overlay naming that moment.
4. Original audio, no trending sound, no voiceover.
5. Caption under six words, plus 5-10 hashtags.

## Hook (first 3 seconds, the opening VISUAL frame)
Camera opens already close on the running motor housing, water audibly moving, and a cat visibly asleep or
fully relaxed within about a foot of it, already in frame. The wow is the juxtaposition itself, motor running
plus a sleeping cat inches away, present from frame one, not built up to.

## Shot list, one unbroken take
| Time | What happens | Camera |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Motor housing running, cat asleep/relaxed close by, both already in frame. | Locked wide-ish angle that holds both the housing and the cat in one shot. |
| 3-5s | A finger enters frame and points at the motor housing itself (not the cat), held for a beat. | Same frame. |
| 5-8s | Bright overlay text appears near the pointed spot, timed to the point. | Same frame, overlay only. |
| 8-11s | Finger stays, camera holds on both housing and sleeping cat, no zoom, no cut. Stop recording. | Same frame throughout. |

audibly loud in the raw footage, do not post this script; the whole premise depends on it being true.

## Format recommendation
Real phone-filmed footage, not AI. Selling "quiet" depends on the viewer trusting what they're looking at;
an AI-generated sleeping cat next to an AI-generated appliance has no way to prove anything about actual
sound level, and the whole claim would read as fabricated the moment it's suspected of being generated.
