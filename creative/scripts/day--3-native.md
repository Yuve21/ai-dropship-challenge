# Day -3 Native Post: "The one entryway fix that actually stopped muddy paw prints"

Account: @dailypawfinds
Protocol: Challenge Warm-Up Protocol v1, Track A, Day -3 (`docs/WARM-UP.md`)
Post type: native, non-promotional. No product, no link, no CTA, no brand pitch of any kind. Post inside the same roughly 2-hour window as the Day -4 post.

## Concept, one line
A home-organization/comfort tip for pet owners: a cheap, already-in-most-homes trick (a towel or mat by the door, positioned a specific way) that catches muddy paws before they hit the carpet.

## Script / shot list (10-13s)

| Time | Shot | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Close on a carpet or rug with an old, faint paw-print stain visible in the corner of frame. | Hook is visual: the viewer immediately clocks "pet owner problem." |
| 2-4s | Wide shot of the front door / entry area as it currently looks. | Sets the scene, no dramatization needed. |
| 4-8s | Demo: hands laying down a towel or absorbent mat directly on the walking path from the door, not off to the side, and smoothing it flat. | The "tip" is the placement (in the direct path, not decorative off to the side), not a product. Any towel or mat works, don't feature a specific branded item. |
| 8-11s | Dog or cat walking in from outside, paws landing on the mat first. | If no live pet walk-through is available, cut to a shot of the mat in place and skip this beat, it's optional. |
| 11-13s | Loop-friendly close: back to the clean carpet corner from the opening shot. | Mirrors the hook shot for a clean loop. |

## On-screen text (burned-in captions)
- 0-2s: "if you have a dog or an outdoor cat you know this stain"
- 4-8s: "put the mat directly in the walking path, not next to it"
- 11-13s: "saved my carpet, no idea why I didn't do this sooner"

## Suggested caption
small fix, big difference. royalty-free audio only, no copyrighted track.

## Why this is genuinely native, not a disguised ad
The tip works with a towel someone already owns. No product is named, shown as a purchase, or implied to be for sale. This is a standard "home tip" format that pet and home-organization creators post constantly, independent of any brand relationship, because it's genuinely useful on its own.

## Format recommendation
**Real phone-filmed UGC.** The credibility of a home tip video comes from it looking like someone's actual entryway and actual mat, not a staged set. AI-generated video would need to fabricate a believable domestic space and a believable pet interaction in the same shot, which is more effort and less convincing than just filming the real thing. This is a 30-second real film, no reason to generate it.

## Compliance notes
- No health/medical claims, no income claims, no before/after framing (the stain shot is context, not a paired before/after transformation edit), no copyrighted music.
- No product, no link, no CTA, no brand mention anywhere in video or caption. Confirmed clean for Day -3.
