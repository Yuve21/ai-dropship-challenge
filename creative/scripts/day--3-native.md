## Concept, one line
A genuinely useful entryway tip that costs nothing: the mat catches paws only if it sits in the actual
walking path from the door, and almost everyone puts it decoratively off to one side instead.

## Hook (first 3 seconds, the opening VISUAL frame)
The doormat as it currently sits, shot from the door looking in, with the obvious walking path running
right past the edge of it. The mistake is visible before any text is read. **Film the real "before" state
of the founder's own door**, do not stage a worse one.

## Script / shot list (11-13s)

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | From the door looking in. Mat sits off to one side. The path past it is obvious. | "if you have a pet, your mat is probably in the wrong place" |
| 2-5s | Hands slide the mat into the direct line from the door into the room and smooth it flat. One continuous shot, no cut. | "it goes in the path, not beside it" |
| 5-8s | Overhead or low angle down the path: door, mat, room, in one straight line. A foot walks the line to show it. | "if they can walk around it, they will" |
| 8-11s | A folded towel tucked under the leading edge of the mat. | "a folded towel under the edge for the wet weeks" |
| 11-13s | Loop-friendly close: back to the exact opening framing, mat now in the path. | "costs nothing. takes ten seconds" |

**Shooting note:** the 2-5s beat is the whole video and it should be **one unedited continuous shot** of
hands moving the mat. That is the physical-product analogue of the Dashboard-and-Pointer mechanic
(`docs/LEARNINGS.md` 2026-08-12): show the thing happening in one take rather than cutting around it. This
is the only beat in the three native posts that gets that treatment, and it is free.
