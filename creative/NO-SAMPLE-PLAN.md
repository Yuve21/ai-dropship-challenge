# The No-Sample Creative Plan: starting Challenge Day 1 with zero product on hand

Written 2026-08-12 by `creative-director`, with `content-engine` and `compliance-guard`, dispatched by
`challenge-lead`. **Supersedes the Challenge Day 1-7 half of `creative/CONTENT-CALENDAR.md`**, whose every
product slot assumes a fountain on camera.

**The decision this plan executes against:** the founder has decided not to order a physical sample and to
start as fast as possible. That is logged in `tracker/LEDGER.md` as a founder decision and it is not
reopened here. What follows is the best version of a no-sample start, not an argument about it. Every
constraint listed below is context so the founder knows exactly what they are holding, and every one of
them has a worked-around slot in the calendar further down.

**What did not change, and cannot:** we do not fabricate. No invented reviews, no invented statistics, no
creator claiming to have used a product nobody has held, no substitute unit filmed as ours. That is the
no-fabrication rule, it is older and wider than the sample rule, and it binds whatever the sample decision
is (`docs/PLAYBOOK.md` "Non-negotiable (account survival)", `.claude/agents/compliance-guard.md`,
`docs/LEARNINGS.md` 2026-08-12).

## 1. The honest constraint list

Six things genuinely cannot be done until a unit is in the house. Stated once, plainly, as facts:

1. **No authentic phone-filmed UGC of the product.** Nothing in frame, ever, until it arrives.
2. **No real cat-and-fountain footage.** No first sip, no reaction shot, no cat choosing it over a bowl.
   That footage was the wow in nine of the twelve scripts already written.
3. **No "I tested this" framing.** No "I've been using it," no "three weeks in," no "it changed her
   habits."
4. **No quality, durability, noise-level, leak-resistance or ease-of-cleaning claims.** We have not heard
   the pump, taken it apart, or left it running overnight.
5. **No answering "does it actually work" from experience,** in a video or in a comment reply. Section 6
   has the prepared honest answer.
6. **No verification that the unit matches the listing photos.** Any image we publish is the supplier's
   representation of the product, not our confirmed one, which changes what we may do with images.

Two consequences worth naming, because they cost real things:

- **The Dashboard-and-Pointer A/B test cannot run as designed.** All three arm-A scripts need the fountain
  doing its trick in one continuous shot (`creative/CONTENT-CALENDAR.md`). The test is deferred, not
  cancelled, and section 7 names the experiment that replaces it this week.
- **Nine existing scripts are unfilmable** and stay in the bank untouched: the three Dashboard-and-Pointer
  scripts, `day3-script-nobody-told-me.md`, `day3-script-bowl-or-fountain-debate.md`,
  `day4-script-pov-still-full.md`, `day4-script-silent-comparison.md`, both full scripts inside
  `day1-hook-bank.md`, and `day--1-product-in-frame.md`. They are correct scripts for the day a unit lands.
  Do not delete them and do not water them down into no-product versions; the no-product versions are the
  new files listed in section 4.

## 2. The viable no-sample formats, ranked

**Read the channel split first, because it decides the ranking and it is the thing most likely to be got
wrong.** Our own evidence points opposite ways for paid and organic:

- **Paid (Meta):** by winner hit rate across 578,750 creatives, **text-only 11.60%, product image plus text
  8.75%, UGC 7.56%, high-production video 6.87%** (Motion, `docs/LEARNINGS.md` 2026-08-10). Statics are
  55.6% of all ads and 64.8% among DTC brands. The all-video assumption is wrong.
- **Organic (TikTok):** video is the only thing that sells, and `.claude/agents/creative-director.md`
  records 300+ scheduled image posts over a year producing nothing. **Statics are not a cheaper substitute
  for organic posting and this plan never uses them as one.**

  (supplier media, generated) sit in the back half, so a slow supplier reply or an unpasted API key costs
  us Day 5-7 slots rather than the launch.

  The fallback is always a text-card hook variant, because that format needs nothing.
- **Family caps are enforced:** Honest Shop (store page and supplier media) posts on Days 3, 5 and 7 only,

  two times of day, plus one voice memo. That is the whole production load for slots 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 8, 11,

## 5. What changes about the Day -1 product-in-frame post (2026-08-17)

**Impossible as written.** `day--1-product-in-frame.md` needs the fountain visible on the counter, running,
as an ordinary object. Nothing is arriving.

**The substitute: a fourth native, non-promotional post.** Full script written:
`creative/scripts/day--1-native-substitute.md` ("The Bowl Nobody Drinks From"), an ordinary observation of
the untouched bowl and where the cat actually drinks, with no product, no pitch, no CTA and no link.

**Why that is the compliant answer.** The Day -1 slot is a step-down, three fully native posts, then one
post where a product merely exists in frame, then promotional content from Day 1. Its purpose is to make
the account's first product-adjacent frame unremarkable. **A fourth native satisfies every rule the slot
imposes and adds none of the risk the slot was managing**, so it is strictly more conservative than the
protocol asks, never less. Posting nothing would drop a scheduled warm-up posting day without cause.

**Explicitly refused:** filming a substitute unit, a competitor's fountain, or a borrowed one and letting it
read as ours. `docs/LEARNINGS.md` (2026-08-12) already ruled that out by name.

**One operational snag, flagged not worked around.** `automation/lib/protocol.js` encodes Day -1 as
`postType: "product-in-frame"` and `automation/cli.js` refuses a `--type` that does not match the day
exactly, so `--type native` on 2026-08-17 **will be refused**. Two clean paths: post that one video by hand
from the phone (which also avoids the reported ~30-50% API reach haircut), or `challenge-lead` decides
whether `protocol.js` should accept a post type strictly more conservative than the day allows. That is a
protocol and code question, not a creative one. **Do not run `warmup skip` to get around it**: that switch
turns off the entire gate and is a decision about the whole protocol, not a workaround for one post type.

## 6. The compliance line, drawn precisely

The governing rule is short: **we may say anything true about our own household, our own store and the
supplier's listing, provided the listing's claims are attributed to the listing. We may not say anything
about the product from experience, because we have none.**

### Phrasings that ARE safe, use these verbatim
- "A lot of cats prefer moving water to still water." (Behaviour observation, already cleared as
  allowed-and-true in `day1-hook-bank.md`.)
- "This is the fountain we picked to sell, and here is why we picked it."
- "Ours has not arrived yet, so there is no demo on this account."
- "The listing says the pump is quiet. I have not heard it."
- "$34.99. 2.2 litres. Plug-in pump. Plastic housing." (Specs quoted from the listing.)
- "Ships in 7 to 20 business days." (`ops/sourcing-dossier.md`, `store/copy/policy-shipping.md`.)
- "That is the supplier's photo, not mine."
- "When it lands, it goes on camera whether it is good or not."
- "I will tell you what I know and what I do not."
- "My cat drinks from the tap and walks past her bowl." (True of our household, no product involved.)

### Phrasings that are NOT safe, do not write these in any form
- "I've been using this for a month." / "Three weeks in and..." / "I tested this." Any personal experience
  with a product nobody has held. **This is the no-fabrication rule, not the sample rule, and it does not
  bend.**
- "My cat drinks twice as much now." / any measured outcome. We have measured nothing.
- "It's completely silent." / "It never leaks." / "Built to last." Quality and durability claims we cannot
  support.
- "4.9 stars, 4,000+ sold." **Those are the supplier's numbers on the supplier's platform.** Repeating them
  in our marketing reads as our store's reviews, of which we have zero. Borrowing a rating is the same
  defect as inventing one.
- "Customers love it" / any testimonial, quote, or star count. We have no customers yet.
- "Vet recommended" / "prevents UTIs" / "supports kidney health" / any hydration-as-medicine framing. Hard
  no on this product regardless of sample status (`compliance/bench-review-2026-08-11.md`, rank 1; Meta and
  TikTok personal-health ad policy).
- "Ships in 3-5 days." Not until a US-warehouse SKU is confirmed. FTC MITOR: ship or notify inside the
  promised window or 30 days.
- "BPA-free." A placeholder, unconfirmed, already forbidden in `store/copy/product-cat-water-fountain.md`
  until a supplier spec sheet exists.
- Any AI-generated image or video of the product presented as a picture of what ships.
- Any other creator's cat footage, or a supplier's video, used without saying whose it is.

### The prepared answer to the question this account will be asked every day
When a comment asks "does it actually work" or "is it loud," reply, verbatim:

> "Honest answer: I do not know yet. Mine has not arrived. The listing says the pump is quiet and I have
> not heard it, so I am not going to tell you it is. When mine lands I will post it either way."

Do not improvise around this. It is the reply that keeps the account honest at the exact moment it is
cheapest to lie.

### AIGC labelling, mechanically
- **Realistic generated video or a synthetic voice: label required.** TikTok auto-detects via C2PA and
  unlabelled realistic AI gets down-ranked or removed (`docs/WARM-UP.md`, documented facts). It is not a
  ranking penalty.
- **`automation/cli.js` defaults `isAigc` to true.** Real footage therefore needs `--no-aigc` passed
  explicitly. **Passing `--no-aigc` on generated content is the violation**; forgetting it on real footage
  is only an inaccuracy. Get both right anyway.
- Slots 12, 15 and 18 are the only labelled posts in this week.

### Changes owed to files that already exist, because the no-sample decision changed what they may claim
1. **`store/copy/product-cat-water-fountain.md`, benefit bullets 2 and 3.** "Genuinely quiet" and "easy to
   take apart and clean, rinse the pump, wipe the basin, back together in under a minute" are written as
   our own verified experience and we have none. Replace with attributed versions before publishing:
   "The listing describes a quiet pump" and "Listed as tool-free to disassemble for cleaning." Or cut them.
   Restore the confident version the day the unit is in hand and confirmed.
2. **`creative/scripts/day1-hook-bank.md`, allowed-claims list.** "The motor is quiet" and "It's easy to
   clean" sit under *Allowed, and true*. Without a held unit they are supplier claims, not our claims. A
   dated amendment note has been added to that file by this run.
3. **`creative/CONTENT-CALENDAR.md`.** Its Day 1-7 rows point at scripts that cannot be filmed. A dated
   superseding note pointing here has been added, so the repo does not carry two live contradictory

## 7. The measurement note: what this costs us against the real-UGC plan

For the ledger, honestly, rather than pretending the two plans are equivalent.

**Paid, using our own documented numbers.** By winner hit rate: text-only **11.60%**, product image plus
text **8.75%**, UGC **7.56%**, high-production video **6.87%**. The no-sample decision removes our ability
to produce the bottom two rows and leaves the top two, which are the two we can build with no product.
**So on the documented axis, the paid creative bank is not degraded by this decision, it is better
composed than the all-video bank we had before.** The genuine paid cost is variety: if supplier permission
fails, spec 2 dies and the bank is a single format, against Meta's and TikTok's own guidance to run 3-5
genuinely different concepts. One format is a thin ad set.

**Organic, where the real cost sits, and there is no number for it.** Nobody has published a winner rate
for organic posts, and we have zero posts of our own, so any percentage quoted here would be invented. The
honest statement is structural:

- The playbook's creative structure is **Hook, Problem, Demo, Benefit, Proof, CTA.** Without the product we
  can produce **Hook, Problem and a claim-free CTA. Three of six beats are gone from every organic post for
  the whole week.** Demo and Proof are the two the playbook calls the wow, and they are exactly the two
  that need the object.
- Our own docs assert, without a number, that **AI video converts below authentic human UGC**
  (`docs/AUTOMATION.md`, `.claude/agents/content-engine.md`). Three of eighteen slots are generated, so
  that penalty applies to one sixth of the week.
- **Do not compute expected organic winners from the 5-8% figure.** That is Meta paid data. Applying it to
  TikTok organic posts would be the exact misuse `docs/LEARNINGS.md` fences external evidence off to
  prevent.

**The two experiments that survive and the one that does not.**
- **Deferred:** the Dashboard-and-Pointer versus standard-UGC A/B for a physical product. Arm A is
  unfilmable. It runs the week after a unit lands, and the calendar already specifies it.
- **Survives intact:** the **comment-bait lever test**. All four prompt shapes (match-my-experience, closed
  poll, open-ended, single-emoji) are spread across the 18 slots and none of them needs the product. This
  is the more valuable of the two levers by the source evidence anyway, a 12x comment-rate spread between
  two posts on the same account (`docs/LEARNINGS.md` 2026-08-12).
- **New, and free:** **real footage versus generated footage**, fifteen real slots against three generated,
  read on hook rate and hold rate. Our own briefs ask for exactly this finding and have never had a way to
  produce it.

**The honest framing for `tracker/LEDGER.md`,** proposed verbatim:

> Days 1-7 organic content ran under a no-sample constraint by founder decision. No post in that week could
> carry a product demo or any experience-based claim, so its hook and hold rates measure problem-side
> creative only and are not comparable like-for-like with post-arrival content. The paid static bank built
> in the same run is not degraded by the constraint: it is composed entirely of the two highest
> winner-hit-rate formats we have evidence for.

**And the one thing this plan genuinely buys us that the real-UGC plan did not:** a week of demand
evidence with the product's execution held constant at zero. If problem-side content with no demo still
pulls buy-intent comments and profile taps, that is a cleaner read on whether people want this category
than a good demo video would have given us, because no part of it can be credited to the creative showing
a nice object. That is a real consolation, not a spin, and it is worth logging as the thing to look at.
