# Sourcing Dossier: Cat Water Fountain + Heated Eye Mask

Status: prep only. No supplier account exists yet, nothing has been ordered or funded. This document
exists so that once a supplier account (AliExpress/DSers, CJdropshipping, or Zendrop) is live and
funded, picking a sample to order is a five-minute decision instead of a cold search.

**Research method and honesty note:** built with WebSearch (most reliable for these platforms tonight)
and direct WebFetch where it worked. AliExpress wholesale *category* pages
(`aliexpress.com/w/wholesale-*.html`) fetched successfully some of the time and returned real listing
data (price, sold count, star rating) shown below. Individual AliExpress *item* pages
(`aliexpress.com/item/...`) consistently redirected into a login wall (`login.aliexpress.com/sync_cookie_read.htm`)
and could not be fetched directly tonight — confirmed, not assumed. CJdropshipping's search/list pages
redirected into a bot-check validation page and could not be fetched directly. Zendrop product pages
require an authenticated session and could not be read. Where a figure below comes from a live fetch or
search snippet, it's marked **(found)**. Where I'm reasoning from general marketplace knowledge because
a live figure wasn't reachable tonight, it's marked **(estimated)**. Shipping costs specifically are
mostly **(estimated)** below because AliExpress folds "free shipping" into the listed item price for
most of these small items and the actual carrier cost isn't broken out on the pages that did load.

## The "order a sample before advertising" rule

This dossier picks *candidates to order*. It is not, and cannot be, a substitute for the project's hard
rule: never advertise a product that hasn't been physically held and checked. Nothing in this document
verifies build quality, actual pump noise level, actual leak resistance, actual heating consistency, or
whether the real item matches the listing photos. All of that only gets confirmed by ordering the sample,
receiving it, and checking it by hand. Treat every number above as a reason to order, not a reason to
skip ordering.
