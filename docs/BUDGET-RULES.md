# Budget Rules — the money discipline

This is the hard constraint of the challenge. `offer-economist` owns it; `challenge-lead` enforces
it; every session updates the ledger.

## The one rule that cannot be broken
- **Out-of-pocket is capped at $100 total, EVER.** Only the founder's first $50-$100 comes from their
  pocket. Track it as a single running number in `tracker/LEDGER.md` (`out_of_pocket_spent`). When it
  hits the cap, no more founder money goes in, full stop.
- **Revenue is not out-of-pocket.** Once customers pay, that money can be reinvested freely (ads,
  stock, tools). Reinvested revenue does NOT count against the $100. Example: put in $80, earn $800,
  spend $600 of it on ads = out-of-pocket is still $80. That is allowed and encouraged.
- If the plan ever "needs" more than $100 of founder money to work, the plan is wrong for this
  challenge. Find a cheaper path (organic) or wait for revenue.

## Suggested allocation of the first $100 (organic-first)
Keep fixed costs near zero so the budget goes to what moves the needle.
- **Store standup: ~$2-15.** Shopify $1/mo promo (first 3 months), domain ~$10-12/yr. DSers free.
  Free Dawn theme. Native reviews. No paid apps, no spy tools at start.
- **Product sample(s): ~$15-30.** Buy the lead product (and maybe a backup) to verify quality and
  film real UGC. Non-negotiable: never advertise a product you have not held.
- **Fulfillment float: ~$30-50 held in reserve.** THIS IS THE CASH-FLOW TRAP. When organic orders
  come in, you must pay the supplier at dispatch, but the processor holds your payout ~3 days (and
  may reserve 5-15%). This reserve fronts your first orders so you never fail to fulfill.
- **Optional first paid test: remainder / early revenue.** Only after a product proves organically.

## Reinvestment ladder (only earned revenue)
1. First profits -> replenish the fulfillment float + faster-shipping SKUs.
2. Then -> a small paid test on the proven winner.
3. Then -> scale paid on winners (<=20% budget bumps), more creative production, a real domain/brand.
4. Only once cash-flow is comfortable -> a spy tool subscription or a private sourcing agent.

## Tracking (update `tracker/LEDGER.md` every session)
Log, dated: money in (founder vs revenue), money out (line-item), out_of_pocket_spent running total,
per-product test result, every kill/scale decision + why, and running gross/net. If a number is
estimated vs confirmed-from-the-dashboard, say which.

## Reserve/hold awareness
Assume you cannot touch a chunk of revenue for up to 90 days on a new store. Do not spend money you
only have on paper. Keep the float. Ramp spend slowly to avoid triggering larger reserves or a
processor review.
