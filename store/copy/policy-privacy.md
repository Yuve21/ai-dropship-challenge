## Information We Collect

**Information you give us directly:**
- Name, email address, shipping address, billing address, and phone number when you place an order
- Email address if you sign up for updates or a newsletter
- Anything you send us directly, like a message through our contact form or email

**Information collected automatically:**
- Device and browser information, IP address, and pages visited, collected through cookies and similar tools when you browse the Site
- Purchase history and cart activity on the Site

We do not collect more than we need to run the store, ship your order, and communicate with you about it.

## How We Use Your Information

- To process and ship your order
- To communicate with you about your order (confirmations, shipping updates, support)
- To respond to questions or requests you send us
- To send marketing emails, only if you've opted in, and you can unsubscribe at any time
- To improve the Site, using aggregate, non-identifying analytics where possible
- To detect and prevent fraud

## Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep your cart working, remember your preferences, and understand how the Site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Turning cookies off may affect how the Site works, including your ability to check out.

## Data Retention

We keep order and account information for as long as needed to fulfill orders, handle returns and refunds, meet legal and tax obligations, and resolve disputes. If you'd like your data deleted sooner, contact us and we'll do so where we're not legally required to keep it.

- **Deletion:** You can request that we delete your personal information, subject to legal recordkeeping requirements (for example, tax records).

## Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we've collected such information, we'll delete it.

## Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information, including relying on Shopify's platform-level security and a reputable payment processor. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent changes. Continued use of the Site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
