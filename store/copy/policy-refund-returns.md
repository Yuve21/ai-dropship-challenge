## Our Policy

We want you to be happy with what you ordered. If something isn't right, here's how returns and refunds work.

**You have 30 days from the date your order is delivered to request a return or refund.**

This 30-day window is the number used consistently across our Shipping Policy, Terms of Service, and product pages. If this number ever changes, it needs to be updated everywhere at once, not just here.

## What's Covered

- **Damaged or defective items:** If your item arrives damaged or doesn't work as described, contact us within 30 days of delivery with a photo or short video of the issue. We'll send a replacement or a full refund, your choice.
- **Wrong item received:** If we sent you the wrong product, contact us and we'll fix it at no cost to you, including return shipping if a return is needed.
- **Changed your mind:** You can return an unused, unopened item within 30 days of delivery for a refund. You're responsible for return shipping costs in this case.

## What's Not Covered

- Items returned after the 30-day window
- Items that show signs of use or damage not caused by a manufacturing defect
- Items missing their original packaging, where packaging is needed to safely process the return

## How to Start a Return or Refund

1. Email us at [PLACEHOLDER: contact email on the real domain] with your order number and a short description of the issue.
2. If the item is damaged, defective, or wrong, include a photo or short video. This helps us resolve it faster and, in many cases, means you won't need to ship anything back.
3. We'll respond within [PLACEHOLDER: X business days, e.g. 1-2] with next steps.
4. Once approved, refunds are issued to your original payment method and typically appear within 5-10 business days, depending on your bank or card provider.

## Return Shipping

- If the return is our error (damaged, defective, or wrong item), we cover return shipping.
- If you're returning an item because you changed your mind, return shipping is your responsibility.
- Because this is a dropshipped item, please contact us before shipping anything back so we can give you the correct return address. Do not send returns to the address on the shipping label.

## Refund Timing

Refunds are processed once we've confirmed the return (or, for damaged/defective items, once we've reviewed your photo/video). After approval, please allow 5-10 business days for the refund to reflect on your statement. Processing times beyond that point are set by your bank or card issuer, not by us.
