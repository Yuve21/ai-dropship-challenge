## Why Cats Love This

Cats are naturally drawn to moving water. It's a well-known trait in cat behavior. A lot of cats will sniff at a still bowl and walk away, but circle back to a fountain because the water is moving, looks fresher, and sounds different.

This fountain uses a quiet pump to keep water circulating all day, so there's always moving water waiting for them, not a bowl that's gone stagnant since this morning.

We're not going to tell you it treats or prevents any health condition. It doesn't, and nobody should claim that. What it does is give your cat a reason to actually visit the water bowl more often.
