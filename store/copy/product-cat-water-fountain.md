## Why Cats Love This

Cats are often drawn to moving water. Plenty of cats will sniff at a still bowl and walk away, then drink
straight from a running tap.

This is a plug-in fountain with a circulating pump, so there is moving water available all day rather than
a bowl that has been sitting since morning.

We are not going to tell you it treats or prevents any health condition. It does not, and nobody should
claim that. What it does is give your cat a reason to visit the water bowl more often.

> **Audit note.** "Cats are naturally drawn to moving water. It's a well-known trait in cat behavior" was
> softened to "often drawn to". The original stated a universal behavioural fact we have no citation for,
> and the tap observation carries the point without it. "Uses a quiet pump" became "a circulating pump".

## Copy Notes for Whoever Pastes This In

- **No health claims.** No UTI prevention, no kidney disease, no hydration framed as a medical benefit.
  "Cats are often drawn to moving water" is a behavioural observation and is the correct framing.
- **No claim stated as our own experience.** Nobody here has held this product. Manufacturer
  specifications may be stated **with attribution**. The same specification stated flatly, as though we
  verified it, is not acceptable. That is the whole line, and it is the one this page previously crossed.
- **Do not invent a supplier name, brand name, or certification.** The BPA-free bullet stays a placeholder
  until confirmed against a real spec sheet.
- **Do not fill the shipping range with a guess.** Pull it from `policy-shipping.md`.
- **No review content** until the reviews app has real reviews in it, and never the supplier's.
- **The capacity in the title is from the sourcing dossier's Option A.** If a different SKU is ordered,
  correct the title in the same session.
- **When a sample is eventually held**, if one ever is, the attributed claims can be promoted to verified
  ones, one at a time, each with a note saying what was checked. Until then attribution stands.
